Privacy Policy

Moj Generator — Last updated: May 13, 2026

Introduction

Cosmic Production d.o.o. ("we", "us", or "our"), a company registered in Split, Croatia, operates the Moj Generator mobile application ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

Data We Handle Locally

Moj Generator stores generator settings, custom lists, and result history locally on your device so the app can remember your choices. We do not operate a user account system or our own backend server for this app.

  • Custom names, list items, dice settings, generated colors, decisions, and result history stay on your device.
  • Deleting the App deletes the locally stored app data from that device.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, custom lists, or generated results on our own servers.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Advertising and Consent

The free version of the App may show ads using Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent management. Google may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data, and diagnostics for advertising, fraud prevention, frequency capping, and measurement, depending on your consent choices and platform settings.

Where required, the App asks for consent before requesting personalized ads. You can manage ad privacy options in the App settings. You can also manage app tracking permission in iOS Settings.

In-App Purchases

The App uses Apple's StoreKit framework for optional in-app purchases. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles payment data.

Data Storage

All generator content that you enter into the App is stored locally on your device. The App uses Apple frameworks for purchases and Google services for ads in the free experience. We do not use a Cosmic Production server to store your generator content.

Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for use by all age groups.

GDPR Compliance

As a company based in the European Union (Croatia), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to access, rectify, or delete. If you have any questions regarding your data rights, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
